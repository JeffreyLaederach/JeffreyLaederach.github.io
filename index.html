In the Beginning


Hi, I'm Jeffrey! You've found my personal website and blog... lucky you! This is where I'll be posting my thoughts, experiences, and insights on various topics that interest me. You can expect to find posts covering a range of subjects including technology, engineering, entrepreneurship, finance, careers, hobbies, personal development, and much more.

Now for a little about me... at the time of this post I am a 3rd year Mechanical Engineering major at Manhattan University in New York City. I will be graduating in May 2027 with a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering with a Concentration in Applied Mathematics and a Minor in Business. Currently, my career has centered around the energy industry specifically in relation to demand-side energy management and building infrastructure solutions. My experience so far covers project management, data analysis, field work, and engineering design. Some of my favorite things are guitars, sports cars, coffee, computers, and lots and lots of Lego. I also love learning new things and dreaming up ways I can apply what I learn to solving real world problems and making the most of my time to make a positive impact.

Thanks for stopping by and I hope you enjoy reading my blog as much as I enjoy writing it!

-Jeff
